Privacy Policy

Bounced · Last updated 30 September 2026

Bounced is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: your game progress is saved on your device (and backed up to our cloud save if you use Game Center), there are no passwords or sign-ups, and ads are provided by Google AdMob.

Your progress and cloud save

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, skins, settings, stats, your player name and any levels you build) is saved on your device. If you are signed in to Game Center, a copy is also backed up to our cloud save (Google Firebase) so it comes back on a new iPhone. The cloud copy is stored under a random account ID linked to your Game Center player, is only used to restore your game, and can only be read by you. It holds no name, email or payment details. Delete it any time in Settings › Delete cloud save, which also deletes the cloud account. If you create a transfer code, the code is shown to you and only goes wherever you choose to paste it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bounced shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional reward videos and ads between levels. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped),
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

If you don't allow tracking, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalised using your IDFA. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later from Settings › Ad privacy choices in the game.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases (coin packs and No Ads) are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. To restore No Ads on a new device, use Settings › Restore purchases.

Game Center leaderboards

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game sends your total stars, highest level, coins earned by playing, weekly challenge results and achievements to Game Center so they appear on the leaderboards, next to your Game Center nickname. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy; we never receive your Apple ID. You can sign out, or limit Game Center for a child, in the iPhone's Settings › Game Center and Screen Time.

Children

Players of all ages, including children under 13, can play Bounced. The first time the game opens it asks what year you were born; it keeps only whether you are under 13, 13–15 or 16+, never the birth year itself.

  • Under 13: ads are requested as child-directed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). They are not personalised, use no advertising identifier (the tracking prompt is never shown) and are limited to general-audience content. Purchases and links out of the game ask a grown-up first.
  • Under 16: ads are not personalised and the tracking prompt is not shown.

We don't collect children's personal information. A child's cloud save holds only game progress, is used only to restore it, and a parent can delete it in Settings › Delete cloud save. Parents who have questions can contact us using the address below.

Deleting your data

Settings › Reset progress erases everything the game has stored on your device. Deleting the app also removes it. To delete the cloud copy, use Settings › Delete cloud save (it also deletes the cloud account), or email us and we will delete it for you.

Changes
