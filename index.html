Common questions

How do I get my progress on a new iPhone?

Sign in to Game Center on both phones. Bounced backs up your progress automatically and brings it back on the new phone. You can also use Profile › Get transfer code on the old phone and Enter transfer code on the new one.

I bought No Ads or coins on another phone

Open Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought them with.

A purchase didn't arrive

Close and reopen the game, then try Settings › Restore purchases. If it's still missing, email us. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Reset progress erases everything on your phone, and Settings › Delete cloud save removes the cloud backup and its account. You can also email us to delete it.

Is Bounced OK for kids?

Yes. Players under 13 get child-directed ads only, are never asked about tracking, and purchases and links ask a grown-up first. Read the privacy policy for details.
